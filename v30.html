How to Play

Quick reference for your next command

01

Choose a card, then a tile

Tap a card in your hand to summon a unit or prepare a spell. Choose a highlighted ground square—not the character artwork—to use it.

02

Move and attack

Select an allied tile. Each ready unit can move once and attack once per turn. Blue tiles are moves, red are attacks, and violet tiles are spells.

03

Use terrain to your advantage

Hold springs for bonus Aeth each turn. Striking downhill from raised ground adds 1 damage. Rocks block ranged attacks.

04

Build your army

Aeth grows across rounds, up to 10 base capacity. Mythic creatures require sacrifices; inspect any card with its i icon for exact details.

05

Win the duel

Reduce the opposing Core from 24 HP to zero. After 24 rounds, score remaining Core HP first, then held springs and surviving units. Exact ties are draws. Online duels are experimental.
